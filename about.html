About Me

I am a Biospecimen Associate II at Guardant Health, where I support high-throughput clinical genomics workflows in a regulated diagnostic environment. My work focuses on maintaining biospecimen integrity, ensuring accurate sample tracking, and adhering to strict quality and chain-of-custody standards that support precision oncology testing.

I earned my B.S. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of California Santa Barbara, with a minor in Anthropology. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted research with Charles Johnson and Enoch Yeung investigating bacterial resistance to antibiotics, which strengthened my foundation in molecular biology and sparked my interest in translating research into clinical impact.

I’m particularly interested in clinical genomics and molecular diagnostics, and in how laboratory science can directly improve patient outcomes through earlier and more accurate disease detection.

As of now, I am based in San Mateo, California. Outside of work, I enjoy building D&D campaigns, writing, coding small projects, and spending time outdoors—activities that reflect my interest in systems, creativity, and problem-solving.
